# Public roadmap

This is a direction for the product, not a delivery commitment. Priorities will change with user feedback.

## Available

- Side-by-side AI image comparisons and model reveal.
- Image and video creation in Studio.
- Projects, generation history, and result sharing.
- Sponsored trial with bounded usage and bring-your-own-key continuation.
- Email/password registration with email verification.

## Next priorities

- Verify the full signup → email verification → trial → own-key journey with real users.
- Improve messages for exhausted trial credit, temporary sponsorship limits, and provider errors.
- Measure where users leave onboarding and whether they complete their first comparison.
- Improve mobile composition and accessibility from observed user sessions.

## Exploring

- Reusable creative briefs and campaign presets.
- More useful comparisons of output quality, generation time, and cost.
- Better reference-image workflows and export options.

Share the problem you want solved in an issue, along with a concrete example.
